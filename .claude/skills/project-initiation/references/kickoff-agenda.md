# Kickoff agenda — align the team to start

Load this to run a project (or phase) kickoff. A kickoff turns an authorized project into
an aligned team: everyone leaves knowing the purpose, the scope boundaries, their role,
and the immediate next steps. Present from `kickoff-deck-outline.md`. Examples use the
running sample project: **Helios**, a customer self-service portal.

## Before the meeting
- Invite the sponsor, the project manager, the core delivery team, and key stakeholders
  identified via `stakeholder-management`.
- Circulate the charter and the agenda in advance so the meeting is for alignment, not
  first reading.
- Pick a length that fits the audience: 30-45 min for a small agile team, 60-90 min for a
  larger waterfall or hybrid program.

## Timed agenda (60 minutes)
| Time | Item | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| 5 min | Welcome and introductions | Who is in the room and their role. |
| 5 min | Why we are here | The business problem and the target outcome (from the charter). |
| 10 min | Objectives and success criteria | What "done" and "good" mean, measurably. |
| 10 min | Scope boundaries | In-scope outcomes and an explicit out-of-scope list. |
| 5 min | Milestones and timeline | High-level dates and the next gate. |
| 5 min | Roles and decisions | Sponsor, PM, RACI at a glance, how decisions get made. |
| 5 min | Delivery approach | Waterfall / agile / hybrid and the governance cadence. |
| 5 min | Risks and assumptions | Top known risks and the biggest assumptions. |
| 5 min | How we will plan | The path to a baseline via `project-planning`. |
| 5 min | Next steps and actions | Owners and dates for the first planning actions. |

## Facilitation tips
- State the out-of-scope list out loud; it prevents the most expensive disputes later.
- Make every action have a single owner and a date before anyone leaves the room.
- If a fundamental disagreement surfaces (e.g. about scope), capture it as an action for
  the sponsor to resolve rather than debating it for the whole meeting.
- For a recurring or running facilitation pattern, see `meeting-management`.

## Outputs to capture
- A short list of actions, each with an owner and a due date.
- Any decisions made and any open questions raised.
- Confirmation of the delivery approach and the next planning step.

## After the meeting
- Send the actions and decisions to attendees within a day.
- Open the planning work by handing off to `project-planning`.
