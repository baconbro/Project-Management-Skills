# Project Closure Report — <Project Name>

Fill this in to formally close a project or phase. Keep it concise — 2-4 pages. It is the
permanent record that the project delivered, was accepted, was handed over, and is being
tracked for benefits. The running example is **Helios**, a customer self-service portal.

- **Sponsor:** _<name>_  |  **Project manager:** _<name>_
- **Closure date:** _<yyyy-mm-dd>_  |  **Report version:** _<v1.0>_

## 1. Summary
- One paragraph: what the project set out to do and the outcome at closure.
- Overall result: _delivered as planned / delivered with changes / cancelled_.

## 2. Objectives and acceptance
- Each objective from the charter and whether it was met.
- Acceptance criteria status; formal acceptance by _<name>_ on _<date>_.
- Accepted exceptions / known defects and their owners.

## 3. Performance against baseline
- **Scope:** delivered vs. baselined; approved changes.
- **Schedule:** planned vs. actual key dates; reasons for variance.
- **Cost:** baseline vs. actual; variance explained. See `cost-management`.

## 4. Handover
- What was handed over (product, environments, docs, support).
- The named operations / run owner who accepted it, and the date.
- Outstanding handover items, if any, with owners.

## 5. Benefits
- Benefits from the `business-case` with target, baseline, and current status.
- The named **benefits owner** and the review dates. See `benefits-realization.md`.

## 6. Risks and issues at closure
- Open risks/issues transferred to the run team, with owners.
- Anything explicitly accepted as residual.

## 7. Lessons learned
- The top lessons (what worked, what did not, what to change). See `lessons-learned`.
- Where each lesson has been routed for reuse.

## 8. Administrative and financial closure
- Issues/actions closed, team released, records archived.
- Contracts and invoices closed; remaining budget released.
- Confirmation that closure checklist items are complete (see `closure-checklist.md`).

## 9. Sign-off
- Sponsor approval of closure: _<name, date>_.
- The project is hereby closed.

---

### How to use this template
1. Pull content from the charter, baseline, issue/risk logs, and business case.
2. Be honest about variance and lessons — this is the record future projects learn from.
3. Replace every _<placeholder>_ and route for sponsor sign-off.
