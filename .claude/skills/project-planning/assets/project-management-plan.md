# Project Management Plan — <Project Name>

Fill this in to produce the integrated plan baselined at the end of planning. Each section
points to the skill that produces its detail; this document integrates them into one
consistent whole. The running example is **Helios**, a customer self-service portal.

- **Sponsor:** _<name>_  |  **Project manager:** _<name>_

## 1. Purpose and objectives
- Business need and target outcome (from the charter).
- Objectives and success criteria (measurable).

## 2. Scope baseline
- In-scope deliverables and the out-of-scope list.
- Link to the WBS / work-package list. See `work-breakdown-structure`.

## 3. Estimates
- Estimating method(s) used and key assumptions. See `estimation`.
- Effort/duration/cost summary by work package.

## 4. Schedule baseline
- Key milestones and dates; the next gate.
- Critical path and major dependencies. See `project-schedule`.

## 5. Cost baseline
- Time-phased budget, contingency, and reserve. See `cost-management`.
- How variance will be tracked in delivery (e.g. CPI/SPI).

## 6. Resource plan
- Who is assigned to what; org/skills needed.
- Capacity check result and any leveling done. See `resource-capacity.md` and
  `resource-allocation.csv`.

## 7. Risk approach
- How risks are identified, scored, owned, and reviewed. See `risk-management`.
- Top risks at baseline.

## 8. Quality approach
- Acceptance criteria, definition of done, quality gates, review cadence. See
  `quality-management`.

## 9. Responsibilities (RACI)
- Who is Responsible/Accountable/Consulted/Informed per major activity. See `raci-matrix`.

## 10. Communications approach
- Audiences, channels, cadence, and escalation path.

## 11. Change control
- How changes to the baseline are proposed, assessed, and approved. See
  `change-management` and `change-request`.

## 12. Baseline sign-off
- Confirm the components are reconciled (see `planning-checklist.md`).
- Record sponsor approval, date, and the frozen baseline (see `baseline-guide.md`).
- Hand off to `project-delivery`.

---

### How to use this template
1. Fill each section by running the linked skill; paste the summary, not the full artifact.
2. Reconcile the sections against each other — budget, schedule, and scope must agree.
3. Replace every _<placeholder>_ before requesting sign-off.
4. Baseline, then hand off to delivery.
